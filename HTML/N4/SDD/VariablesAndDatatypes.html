What is a variable?

Think of a variable as a digital storage box inside a computer's memory. Instead of just throwing information everywhere, you give the box a specific name so you can find it later. Inside that box, you can keep all sorts of things—like a high score, someone's name, or a simple "yes/no" answer.

In the world of coding, variables are a total game-changer. They allow a programme to remember things and change them on the fly. Without them, software would be pretty boring and rigid; it would only ever be able to do one specific thing. Because of variables, your favourite games and apps can react to what you do, keep track of your progress, and work differently every time you use them.
