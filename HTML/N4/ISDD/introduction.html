What are Information Systems?

An information system a way of digitally storing and organising information and data. Websites are a formed of information system, because the information on a website is sorted into files and folders so that people can easily find and access it. Databases are also a common type of information system, since they are tools specifically created for storing data, sorting it and searching through it. Many services combine both, with people using a website to manipulate a database that works in the background. Here are some examples of information systems:

  • Our school uses an information system to store data on all the pupils who go to school here. It's called SEEMiS.
  • The police use an information system to store data on all of the people with criminal records that they are aware of.
  • Wikipedia is an information system that stores data on a large variety of topics that can be accessed by anyone.
  • Facebook is an information system that has a largey variety of user data stored in its database. This includes usernames, email addresses, and passwords.
  • Even your phone's address book is an information system. It stores contact details on people that you know!

Because so many people use them, information systems have become a big part of our everyday lives.

In this unit, we will be looking at two different kinds of Information Systems: Websites and Databases.

Data and Information

We have often heard the terms data and information. They are are often used interchangeably, so people assume that they are the same thing. They are not! Here's the difference:

  • Data is raw facts and figures that are collected and stored. It is the basic building blocks of information. For example, the number of apples sold in a store each day is data - it is just a number.
  • Information is data that has been processed, analysed, and presented in a way that makes it useful and meaningful. For example, if we take the data on the number of apples sold each day and calculate the average number of apples sold per day, we have created information.

In short, information is data that people have assignment a meaning to.
